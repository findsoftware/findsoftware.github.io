# find.software advisory board

Our project is funded by the DFG as part of the [“Research Software Infrastructures” funding program](https://www.dfg.de/en/research-funding/funding-opportunities/programmes/infrastructure/lis/funding-opportunities/research-software-infrastructures). Our goal is to establish find.software as an infrastructure. There are many hurdles to overcome, and sound advice from experienced individuals and projects in this field is very helpful. To ensure sustainability and community involvement from the very beginning, we decided to establish an [advisory board](../../about/advisory-board.md). The [inaugural meeting](../../about/advisory-board.md#meetings) took place on November 5, 2026, and we are very pleased to welcome [three experienced founding members](../../about/advisory-board.md#current-members) for our advisory board: Daniel Garijo (Universidad Politécnica de Madrid), Deepesha Burse (Wikimedia Germany), and Florian Mannseicher (deRSE).

Daniel is a recognized expert in the field of software metadata and a long-standing member of the [CodeMeta Initiative](https://codemeta.github.io/) as well as the [SciCodes Consortium](https://scicodes.net/). Deepesha is a developer advocate at [Wikimedia Germany](https://www.wikimedia.de/) and serves as our liaison to both the developers behind Wikidata and the community. Florian, in turn, represents [deRSE](de-rse.org), the German Society for Research Software, which will be a valuable partner in our efforts to raise the profile of research software.

Here you’ll find the [advisory board agreement](../../supplements/Advisory-board-agreement.pdf), the [list of board members](../../about/advisory-board.md#current-members), and the [minutes from our first meeting](../../supplements/20261105-Inaugural-meeting-protocol.pdf). 

We’re very much looking forward to continuing our collaboration and are really excited to see what it’s like to work with an advisory board on this project ;)
